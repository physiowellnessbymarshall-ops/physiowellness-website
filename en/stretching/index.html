What is Stretching

A guided space to move with greater freedom

Stretching is guided mobility and flexibility bodywork, tailored to how your body moves and feels in each session. Rather than a rigid stretching chart identical for everyone, it is calibrated to your structure, rhythm, and daily needs.

It is not about forcing the body; it is about reclaiming space to move.

Who it is for

Does it suit you?

Four common situations where developing mobility and releasing muscular tension makes a tangible difference to your daily life.

It may be right for you if...

  • Feeling of stiffness
  • Long hours sitting
  • Improving your mobility
  • Active individuals or athletes
  • Complementing other treatments or workouts

Physio Wellness Philosophy

It is not about stretching further. It is about moving better.

At Physio Wellness, we know the body does not respond to excessive strain. Stretching is neither a generic routine nor a succession of forced positions: it is a guided process rooted in observing exactly how your structure responds in each session.

Areas of focus

What we address in each session

Each session combines several of these objectives based on how you arrive, balancing release, mobility, and control.

01

Mobility

Expands joint range of motion so daily movement feels effortless and light.

We work through hips, shoulders, ankles, and spine along their natural axes, reducing articular friction and encouraging fluid everyday movement.

02

Flexibility

Builds muscular suppleness progressively and tailored to your baseline.

Conscious elongation of anterior and posterior muscle chains, respecting comfort thresholds without provoking bounce or reflex guarding.

03

Full range of motion

Develops full expression through each gesture without pushing past safe boundaries.

We work to reclaim degrees of freedom at end-ranges where motor control is typically lost, enhancing safety in any movement.

04

Muscular tension

Releases tension accumulated from daily work, sport, or prolonged postures.

Deactivating contractures and strain in the neck, back, and legs through diaphragmatic breathing and guided, rhythmic traction.

05

Body recovery

Accelerates recovery between strength, Pilates, or athletic workouts.

Stimulates tissue oxygenation and restores resting neuromuscular tone to support optimal, faster physical recovery.

The flow

A session shaped by where you are today

Three coordinated stages ensuring every minute has a clear purpose and tangible benefit.

01

Stage 01

We observe

How you arrive, how you move, and what needs attention.

We assess overall stiffness, areas of accumulated tension, and joint ranges before commencing movement.

02

Stage 02

We work

Mobility, flexibility, breathing, range of motion, or tension release based on your needs.

Applying assisted stretching, gentle traction, active joint mobilisations, and fascial work tailored to the findings.

03

Stage 03

We integrate

Ensuring movement gains translate into everyday ease and continuity.

Consolidating bodily sensations with practical, mindful cues so you maintain suppleness in your routine or workouts.
