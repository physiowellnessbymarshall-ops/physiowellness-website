The Marshall Method

We don't start where it hurts. We start by understanding why.

A proprietary methodology in Sitges transforming isolated sessions into a continuous journey of clinical assessment, manual therapy, and guided movement.

THE CORE PRINCIPLE

Every body tells a different story.

That is why our care is tailored to you.

We do not believe in prefabricated workout cards or ten-minute automated protocols. The pain you feel today is the culmination of adaptations, compensations, and habits formed over time. To resolve it at its core, we integrate five coordinated clinical stages:

01

PHASE 01 · STARTING POINT

Listen

The full story, not merely the immediate symptom.

We begin by listening unhurriedly to your story, your pain, and your goals. The discomfort you feel rarely originates where it surfaces; it is often the final signal of accumulated load, postural stress, or unresolved past injuries.

Setting: Consultation Room · Sitges
  • Clinical interview Unhurried and attentive
  • Daily lifestyle Routines, work, and sports
  • Objective Clarity and direction
Active listening and clinical dialogue Understanding your full history before any intervention.
01 → 02
02

PHASE 02 · CLINICAL PRECISION

Assess

A thorough clinical assessment to determine your true baseline.

We transition to rigorous physical examination: measuring joint range of motion, muscle activation, and compensatory habits through targeted orthopaedic tests. We bring objective biomechanical insight to replace uncertainty.

Discipline: Clinical Physiotherapy · Joint Assessment
  • Joint mobility End-feel and range tests
  • Motor control Compensatory pattern screening
  • Clinical rationale Root cause vs. referred pain
Biomechanical assessment Measuring joint ranges and compensations to act with precision.
02 → 03
03

PHASE 03 · TARGETED INTERVENTION

Treat

Targeted intervention using the right tools for your specific condition.

We act directly upon the dysfunctional or injured tissue. Combining advanced orthopaedic manual therapy, joint mobilisation, neuromodulation, or myofascial release, every intervention has a precise clinical purpose: reduce irritation, release tension, and restore balance.

Discipline: Physiotherapy & Advanced Manual Therapy
  • Manual therapy Targeted hands-on relief
  • Technology Evidence-based clinical tools
  • Outcome Pain modulation & tension relief
Manual therapy ortopédica Calm irritation, mobilize tissue, and alleviate acute pain.
03 → 04
04

PHASE 04 · ACTIVE RE-EDUCATION

Guide

We guide you as you rebuild mobility, strength, and confidence.

The treatment table helps relieve acute pain, but active movement is what solidifies lasting recovery. In this phase, we move beyond passive care to teach your body to move without fear: pairing the postural precision of the Pilates Reformer with controlled resistance from therapeutic strength training.

Disciplines: Pilates Reformer + Functional Strength
  • Pilates Reformer Alignment & fluid resistance
  • Tailored strength Progressive load & control
  • Confidence Overcoming fear of movement
From the table to active movement Active re-education with Pilates Reformer and dosed therapeutic exercise.
04 → 05
05

PHASE 05 · SUSTAINABLE AUTONOMY

Evolve

Continuing to develop your gains, at your pace over time.

True success in the Marshall Method is not clinical dependence, but your personal autonomy in caring for your body. We incorporate deep Stretching and Wellness techniques so you establish lasting habits and maintain vitality.

Disciplines: Assisted Stretching + Wellness / Self-Care
  • Clear guidance Practical tools for daily life
  • Resilience Active prevention of recurrence
  • Independence You take control of your health
Autonomy and mindful self-care Autonomous habits and tools empowering you to direct your own well-being.

MOVEMENT WITH PURPOSE

The body is designed for movement, not immobilisation

In the Marshall Method, movement is not an optional afterthought. It is the primary therapeutic tool that re-educates the nervous system and cements clinical gains.

Mobility

Restoring natural joint range without pinching or restriction, enabling fluid, pain-free movement.

Control

Stabilizing your core before demanding load. Deep neuromuscular control coordinates the right muscles at the right moment.

Capacity

Calibrating progressive load so tendons, ligaments, and muscles withstand the real demands of work, leisure, and sport.

Our goal is not just returning to where you were.

Our goal is helping you understand how to advance.

CLINICAL APPLICATION

How the Marshall Method unfolds in your initial session

Methodology comes to life in the consultation room. When you enter Physio Wellness in Sitges, here is the tangible protocol you will experience.

01 Minutes 00–20

1-on-1 consultation & functional assessment

We review your clinical history, examine previous medical tests, and perform mobility and pain evaluations in a private setting.

02 Minutes 20–50

Direct hands-on treatment on examination table

Immediate treatment addressing the issue. You do not leave with only an opinion: you receive hands-on care in the very first visit.

03 Minutes 50–60

Clear explanation & personalised plan

We explain what is happening in accessible language, establish realistic milestones, and agree on next steps without filler sessions.
