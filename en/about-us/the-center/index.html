ONE SHARED APPROACH

Two spaces, because the entire journey does not happen in one place.

The road to solid recovery is not linear, nor does it fit into a single room. Some moments demand quiet reflection, attentive clinical listening, and hands-on therapy on an examination table.

Other moments call for standing up: reactivating muscles, regaining confidence in movement with the Pilates Reformer, and building strength so pain does not return.

This is why Physio Wellness features two adjoining spaces in Sitges. They are not separate entities: they are two environments created to let your progress unfold naturally, seamlessly, and under the same clinical guidance.

01 · Clinical Space & Assessment

A sanctuary to pause, observe, and understand.

A quiet, private room designed for unhurried clinical listening without distractions. Here you have dedicated time to share your case, investigate root causes, and receive advanced manual therapy on a hydraulic table with calibrated lighting.

01

Individual diagnostic assessment

Comprehensive assessment of mobility, discomfort, and biomechanical history without rushing.

02

Specific manual treatment

Joint mobilisation, myofascial techniques, and neuromuscular relief on a specialised hydraulic table.

03

Continuous clinical follow-up

Periodic re-evaluation of tissue response to calibrate treatment intensity.

04

Personalised 1-on-1 care

A full hour dedicated entirely to you with your primary physiotherapist.

02 · Active Movement Studio

A space designed to move with confidence again.

A bright, spacious studio adjacent to the physiotherapy room. Equipped with tower Pilates Reformers and a functional strength training area. Here we dispel the fear of movement, moving from passive relief on the table to real physical capacity under professional supervision.

01

Pilates Reformer with tower

Postural alignment, joint decompression, and core stability guided by a calibrated spring system.

02

Functional strength & rehabilitation

Force USA multi-rack, adjustable bench, and progressive overload adapted to your threshold.

03

Movement re-education

Learning safe motor patterns under constant guidance to rebuild movement confidence.

04

Active training to reduce relapse risk

The essential bridge solidifying table treatment and cultivating lasting physical autonomy.

Two adjoining rooms with unified clinical criteria: the natural transition from the table to guided exercise.

CONTINUITY & COMMUNICATION

From the table to active movement, without losing continuity.

Both spaces sit side-by-side. The physiotherapist and movement coach share the same medical history and standards, making your transition smooth and continuous.
