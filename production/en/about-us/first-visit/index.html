Step-by-step journey

What to expect in your first visit

Here is what happens from the moment you step through our doors: a clear, progressive process designed to provide certainty.

Phase 01 · Welcome & Conversation
WHAT TO EXPECT

An unhurried conversation in a calm setting

We begin seated, discussing your situation. This is never a rushed form-filling exercise; we focus on understanding when pain began, how it affects sleep, work, or sports, and your personal expectations.

Private setting Active listening Without preconceptions
Phase 02 · Clinical Examination
WHAT TO EXPECT

Joint measurement and root-cause localization

We move to physical examination: assessing joint mobility, muscle tone, and specific orthopaedic tests. We explain each maneuver so you understand what we are assessing throughout.

Orthopaedic tests Joint range of motion Manual palpation
Phase 03 · Active Movement
WHAT TO EXPECT

Observing how your body responds in action

Pain rarely presents the same at rest as in motion. We ask for functional movements, everyday postures, or gentle tests to evaluate motor control and uncover protective compensations.

Motor control Active patterns Compensatory screening
Phase 04 · Immediate Intervention
WHAT TO EXPECT

Targeted hands-on treatment from day one

Whenever assessment indicates it is appropriate and safe, we provide treatment during this initial visit: applying manual therapy, gentle mobilizations, or myofascial work to relieve pain and improve comfort immediately.

Manual therapy Symptom relief Evidence-based care
Phase 05 · Guidance & Next Steps
WHAT TO EXPECT

Total clarity and practical home guidance

We conclude by answering all your questions. You leave with a transparent explanation of your condition, practical guidance for daily life, and an honest roadmap forward.

Home guidance No forced packages Tailored plan

In practice

An initial consultation designed to understand before deciding.

The 60 minutes of your first visit are structured so nothing is left to chance, combining dialogue, clinical rigor, and immediate treatment.

1. Clinical dialogue & history

We analyze your discomfort alongside your lifestyle context: work ergonomics, athletic habits, past unresolved injuries, and physical demands.

2. Biomechanical & joint assessment

Through diagnostic palpation and orthopaedic tests, we measure mobility ranges, muscle tone, and tissue sensitivity to locate the true root cause rather than just where it aches.

3. Functional movement screening

We observe your response to load, stability, and coordination in real movement, identifying asymmetries or compensations that sustain the issue.

4. Initial care & self-management

We deliver manual therapy to modulate discomfort and provide clear self-care and ergonomic guidance so you know what to do and what to avoid.

Continuity

What happens after the first visit?

Every journey progresses differently. Following initial assessment, we agree upon a path that makes clinical sense for your recovery, free from rigid routines or unnecessary sessions.

01

Clinical follow-up

Individual 50 or 30-minute manual therapy sessions to modulate pain, adapt load, and evaluate progress.

02

Movement & Strength

Guided physical conditioning in our strength room to restore load tolerance, consolidate mobility, and reduce the risk of relapse.

03

Pilates Reformer

Core motor control, postural alignment, and joint stability supported by springs on hardwood Reformers.

04

Guided stretching

Guided passive mobilizations and stretches to increase range of motion and release tension.

05

Personal autonomy

If your issue resolves quickly or only requires preventive care, we equip you with clear exercises to continue independently.
