THE FIVE DISCIPLINES

Five areas that can be part of the same process

Each area addresses a distinct moment in your body's journey. They are not isolated silos: they share the same clinical team, the same records, and an integrative evaluation adapted to your progress.

01Physiotherapy → 02Strength → 03Pilates → 04Wellness → 05Stretching
01

01 — CLINICAL ASSESSMENT & TREATMENT

Recover, treat, and return to moving better

Physiotherapy is the starting point when facing pain, an injury, or physical limitation in your daily life or sport. We carry out an exhaustive assessment to identify the biomechanical root cause, combining hands-on manual therapy, clinical technology, and therapeutic exercise tailored to you.

Services included in this area

Orthopaedic physiotherapy Sports physiotherapy Pelvic floor rehabilitation Neurological & oncology physiotherapy Dermatofunctional physiotherapy In-clinic and home visit options
Explore Physiotherapy
02

02 — TISSUE CAPACITY & PERFORMANCE

Develop strength, physical capacity, and performance

Strength training guided by clinical criteria is the cornerstone of lasting recovery. We guide each session with individualized loading so your muscles and joints comfortably tolerate the demands of your lifestyle or sport without relapse.

Services included in this area

1-on-1 individualized strength training Sports physical rehabilitation Motor control & active mobility under load Injury prevention & joint longevity
Explore Strength
03

03 — REFORMER BIOMECHANICS

“A guided approach to how you move, not a generic group class.”

Enhance control, mobility, stability, and alignment

The Reformer uses graduated spring resistance and a moving carriage to provide postural support or challenge neuromuscular control. We focus on core stability, spinal decompression, and precision in every joint.

Services included in this area

1-on-1 private Reformer session Postural control & deep core stabilization Spinal mobility & biomechanical breathing
Explore Pilates
04

04 — ACTIVE RECOVERY & BODY CARE

Care for, restore, and optimize the body

We consider wellness an active discipline of health recovery: a dedicated space to relieve muscular overload, accelerate tissue regeneration, and rebalance the nervous system following intense physical strain or prolonged work stress. Not a spa: evidence-based body restoration.

Services included in this area

Deep therapeutic massage Manual lymphatic drainage Reboots sports compression therapy Mito Light red light therapy Theragun percussive therapy
Explore Wellness
05

05 — FLEXIBILITY & RANGE OF MOTION

Gain flexibility and freedom of movement

Guided stretching helps reclaim joint space lost to sedentary work, poor posture, or repetitive strain. Without aggressive or painful forcing, we gently guide your muscles to relax and expand your range of motion safely and progressively.

Services included in this area

Assisted table-guided stretching Active and passive joint mobility Myofascial tension release Integrated with Strength or Physiotherapy
Explore Stretching

STILL HAVE QUESTIONS?

You don't need to know what treatment you need before coming.

The 60-minute initial consultation is designed specifically for this: to listen to you, evaluate your movement with clinical rigor, and decide on the most sensible next step together.

You don't have to arrive with a predetermined choice or medical term. Simply tell us what you feel, when the discomfort began, or what goal you want to achieve.

INITIAL CLINIC VISIT · 60 MINUTES

What happens during your first visit

  1. 01
    01 · Active listening

    In-depth review of your symptoms, injury history, and daily and athletic habits.

  2. 02
    02 · Assessment

    Clinical diagnostic assessment and functional joint movement tests to identify biomechanical causes.

  3. 03
    03 · Clear diagnosis

    Clear, straightforward explanation of what is happening in your body, free of confusing jargon.

  4. 04
    04 · First intervention

    Hands-on initial therapy or adapted exercise within the same session to begin relief immediately.

  5. 05
    05 · Continuity plan

    Practical home exercises and a realistic, transparent continuity strategy adapted to your goals.
