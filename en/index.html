It is not always easy to know where to begin

You may arrive with pain, fatigue, a desire to rebuild strength, or simply the feeling that your body needs to move better. Let's start with what you need today.

I have pain, an injury, or need to recover function. Physiotherapy I want to care for my body, recover from exertion, or reduce fatigue. Wellness I want to build strength, enhance capacity, or perform better. Strength I want to move with greater control, mobility, and stability. Pilates I want to increase flexibility, release tension, and expand range of motion. Stretching

Not sure which is right for you? We will guide you during your first visit.

One journey, five moments of light

First visit

60 min · One-to-one session

Your first visit begins by understanding you

A 60-minute individual session to understand your case, assess your starting point, and determine how to begin.

Reception area · Physio Wellness
Book first visit We can help on WhatsApp
  1. 01

    We discuss your case

    Without rushing. We listen to your history, your pain, your physical sensations, and what you need to recover.

  2. 02

    We assess your starting point

    A precise clinical and biomechanical examination to identify the true root of your discomfort.

  3. 03

    We define clear objectives

    We explain what is happening and outline a transparent plan tailored to you.

  4. 04

    We perform an initial intervention

    We begin treatment from the very first session using manual therapy or appropriate techniques.

  5. 05

    Recommendations and next steps

    You leave with clear guidelines for your daily life and complete clarity on the process.

I am a visual artist, and my profession requires maintaining a tense and demanding posture on a constant basis, and has done so for several decades.

I came to Marçal Roig Ramírez due to neck and shoulder pain that had become constant. Imaging tests showed nothing out of the ordinary, yet the pain persisted.

Initially, Marçal worked to relax the musculature through various massage techniques and, following his recommendations, we began an intensive programme of exercises and strength training for muscular development.

After a year of work and effort, I can say that, thanks to his professional expertise, his dedication and his consistency, I have completely freed myself from pain.

Marçal possesses an extraordinary understanding of how the human body works, steady hands and a charming, deeply kind personality. His professionalism, his dedication and his profound knowledge of the body make you feel you are in the very best hands.

Marçal, thank you so much for everything.
