How we can help

You do not need to know which type of physiotherapy you require

Select what you are experiencing to see the clinical discipline best suited to address it.

  • You are experiencing pain or discomfort affecting your daily activities, even without knowing the exact cause.

    Related discipline

    General physiotherapy. Clinical assessment and treatment of daily pain or functional limitations.

  • You want to restore mobility following an injury, surgery, or musculoskeletal condition.

    Related discipline

    Orthopaedic physiotherapy. Rehabilitation after injury, surgery, or musculoskeletal conditions.

  • You want to work on mobility, coordination, and functional capacity related to a neurological condition.

    Related discipline

    Neurological physiotherapy. Movement therapy and functional training for neurological conditions.

  • During or following cancer treatment, you are seeking supportive physiotherapy tailored to your pace.

    Related discipline

    Oncological physiotherapy. Compassionate physiotherapy care during or after oncology treatment, attuned to your rhythm.

  • You are looking for a discreet, unhurried space to assess and treat the pelvic floor musculature.

    Related discipline

    Pelvic floor therapy. Specific evaluation and rehabilitation of pelvic floor muscles in complete privacy.

  • Your skin or connective tissue requires specialized clinical physiotherapy care.

    Related discipline

    Dermatofunctional physiotherapy. Specialized physiotherapy focused on skin, scars, and connective tissue.

  • Assessment and treatment of daily pain or functional limitations.

  • Rehabilitation following injury, surgery, or musculoskeletal conditions.

  • Movement and functional training for neurological conditions.

  • Supportive physiotherapy during or after oncological treatment, tailored to your rhythm.

  • Evaluation and treatment of pelvic floor musculature in a calm, private setting.

  • Physiotherapy treatment dedicated to skin and connective tissue.

Your first visit, step by step

The first step is understanding what is holding you back

Before treatment begins, we conduct a comprehensive initial assessment: where it hurts, how it restricts you, and what you wish to achieve. From there, your care may combine manual therapy, movement, exercises, and tailored guidance.
