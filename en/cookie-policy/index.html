Cookie Policy

What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small data file stored on a user's terminal device (computer, tablet, or smartphone) to store and retrieve information about their visit to the Website. Cookies are downloaded by your browser when you access the Website. In this Cookie Policy, the term "cookie" refers to all files that store information in this manner.

Cookies serve various purposes, including recognizing your device on return visits, ensuring that content loads correctly, and saving your preferences. Cookies do not harm your device.

What types of cookies do we use?

Cookies can be distinguished by their purpose, duration, and the entity that manages them. A single cookie may fall into multiple categories:

By purpose

1. Technical cookies

Technical cookies enable users to navigate the Website and access its features and services. Some are essential for the operation of the site, such as maintaining active sessions or managing security. If technical cookies are disabled, parts of the Website may not function properly.

2. Analytics and statistical cookies

Analytics cookies gather aggregate information about visits to evaluate Website usage, analyze navigation patterns, and monitor performance. Data gathered may include your IP address, browser type, date and time of visits, and visited pages, used strictly for statistical and security purposes.

3. Behavioural advertising cookies

Behavioural advertising cookies store information on user behaviour obtained through ongoing observation of browsing habits. This enables the display of relevant advertising aligned with a user's interests.

By managing entity

1. First-party cookies

Cookies sent to your device from a domain or equipment managed directly by PHYSIO WELLNESS to deliver the requested service.

2. Third-party cookies

Cookies sent to your device from a domain or equipment managed by a third party that processes data obtained through cookies (such as analytics providers or advertising networks).

By duration

1. Session cookies

Cookies designed to collect and store data temporarily while you access and browse the Website.

2. Persistent cookies

Cookies where data remains stored on your device and can be accessed and processed for a defined period, ranging from minutes to several years.

What cookies will you find on our website?

PHYSIO WELLNESS uses first-party technical cookies (to enable secure browsing) and third-party cookies, including Google Analytics (to analyze site traffic and user behavior) and Meta/Facebook (for conversion tracking and marketing where applicable).

How to configure, disable, and block cookies?

You can configure or withdraw your cookie consent at any time through our cookie preference settings on the Website.

Additionally, you can configure your browser to accept, block, or delete cookies. If you choose to disable certain cookies, your browsing experience may be affected. To manage cookie settings in your browser, refer to the following official guides:

  • Chrome: https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647
  • Firefox: https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/enhanced-tracking-protection-firefox-desktop
  • Safari: https://support.apple.com/en-gb/guide/safari/sfri11471/mac
  • Microsoft Edge: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-edge/delete-cookies-in-microsoft-edge

This Cookie Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in legislation, regulatory guidance, or our cookie usage. We encourage you to review it regularly.
