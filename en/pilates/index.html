What is Pilates Reformer

Guided movement, not a generic class

Unlike mat exercises, the Reformer incorporates calibrated spring resistance, a gliding carriage, and pulleys to provide biomechanical support or create a precise stimulus for strength and motor control.

It is not about keeping up with a group choreography or forcing postures: we guide every movement so your body learns to move with fluidity, stability, and without unnecessary strain.

01 Control

Active movement awareness and alignment throughout every phase of the exercise.

02 Resistance

Progressive elastic tension that assists or challenges with precision and smoothness.

03 Precision

Tailored adjustments in every movement arc adapted to your unique biomechanics.

Precision and biomechanical support

The core tool: Pilates Reformer

The machine does not dictate the exercise: we calibrate spring tension, carriage travel, and pulleys to your anatomy, starting point, and goals.

Core of the method

Pilates Reformer

Millimetre movement control and elastic assistance

Unlike mat work, the Reformer introduces graded spring tension, a gliding platform, and drive straps. This allows us to de-load body weight on sensitive joints or deliver precise stimuli for deep strength and stability.

01

Elastic resistance

Calibrated springs support muscular contraction without joint impact or overload.

02

Joint support

The gliding carriage supports the body and guides movement paths, easing pressure on the spine and knees.

03

Active alignment

Pulleys demand symmetrical motor control, reinforcing core stability across every phase of movement.

Starting point

You don't need prior Pilates experience to start

Every session is individualised from your very first consultation. There is no minimum fitness level required.

Learning to move with calm, confidence, and without preconditions.

You don't need prior experience or exceptional flexibility. The session is completely tailored to where you are today. The Reformer supports your body, allowing you to feel movement clearly and safely.

✓ One-to-one guidance for every repetition and posture.
✓ The machine assists and eases pressure on sensitive areas.
✓ A measured pace where quality always comes before quantity.

Deepening control, core stability, and movement quality.

Pilates can be used to refine control, mobility, and movement quality as a complement to your strength training or sport. It helps identify imbalances and enhance efficiency in every movement.

✓ Identifying and actively addressing biomechanical asymmetries.
✓ Eccentric strengthening and lumbo-pelvic stabilisation.
✓ Direct transfer to your primary sport or training programme.

An active bridge to consolidate recovery and regain function.

Reformer Pilates can be integrated into a broader rehabilitation plan when appropriate. If you are recovering from an injury, the Reformer enables assisted active movement before returning to conventional loads.

✓ Direct coordination with the physiotherapy team at Physio Wellness.
✓ Safe neuromuscular stimulation without joint impact.
✓ Long-term continuity to maintain and reinforce your recovery.

Session breakdown

What a session looks like

Three structured phases designed to ensure every minute has purpose and adapts to your needs.

Phase 01

Starting point

We begin by understanding how you move and what you need today. We discuss your current sensations, recent discomfort, or fatigue to direct the focus of the session.

Phase 02

Guided practice

We select exercises and resistance levels tailored to you. Your instructor guides each repetition, ensuring alignment, tempo, and breathing at every step.

Phase 03

Progression

The work evolves as your control, mobility, and strength develop. We conclude with integration, calm, and practical takeaways for your daily life.
