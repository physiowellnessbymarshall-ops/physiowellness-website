A Physiotherapy modality

An individualised process, without the need to travel

If you cannot travel to the centre or prefer receiving care at home, home physiotherapy maintains the same clinical standards, rigor, and personalised attention as our in-clinic consultations.

This is neither an isolated treatment nor a sporadic emergency call: it is a comprehensive therapeutic process, structured to understand your starting point, relieve pain, restore mobility, and ensure steady progress in your own surroundings.

01

Initial assessment

We identify the root cause of your symptoms, evaluating your mobility and functional limitations within your daily home environment.

02

Adapted treatment

We deliver hands-on manual therapy, joint mobilisation, and active exercise using equipment brought directly to your home.

03

Continuous follow-up

We provide specific recommendations and monitor your progress session by session to consolidate functional improvements.

Common situations

When does home physiotherapy make sense?

Home care is designed for specific situations where travelling compromises your comfort, safety, or clinical recovery.

  • Temporary or chronic difficulty walking, climbing stairs, or travelling safely and independently.

  • Early phases following surgery where relative rest is required and avoiding vehicle journeys or uncomfortable waits is a priority.

  • Acute episodes of lower back pain, sciatica, or joint stiffness where moving outside aggravates symptoms.

  • Gentle, patient-centred care in familiar surroundings, focusing on functional mobility, transfers, and safety within the home.

  • Sprains, healing fractures, or trauma where avoiding unnecessary weight-bearing during the initial days is advisable.

  • Demanding schedules, family commitments, or simply the preference of receiving your session with complete privacy and comfort.

Professional equipment

Everything required travels with us.

You do not need any special equipment at home. We bring the professional instruments required to ensure a safe and comfortable treatment.

01

Functional assessment

Orthopaedic tests, precise goniometry, and neuromuscular response examination to identify the source of your limitation.

02

Ergonomic portable treatment table

Height-adjustable clinical table with padded support and facial cutout to ensure postural comfort during treatment.

03

Manual therapy & Hygiene

Neutral therapeutic-grade creams, single-use towels and sheets to meet the highest standards of clinical hygiene.

04

Mobility & guided exercise

Controlled resistance bands, proprioception accessories, and exercises adapted to the everyday furniture in your home.

05

Follow-up guidelines

Clear self-care guide, ergonomic advice, and specific recommendations between visits.
