What is Wellness

Targeted bodywork, not a day-spa treatment

At Physio Wellness, we approach wellness as an active discipline for health and performance: a dedicated space to reduce muscle tension, promote tissue regeneration, and restore functional balance to your body.

It is not a substitute for clinical assessment when addressing an acute injury; rather, it serves as a specialised discipline to enhance recovery, complement your training, or integrate structured physical care into your weekly routine.

Wellness goals

What does your body need today?

Four targeted pathways tailored to where you are right now, your fatigue levels, and the physical demands of recent days.

Methodology and technology

Tools for each objective

We first define the specific benefit your body requires, then select the appropriate hands-on techniques and clinical technologies to achieve it.

01

Body goal

Recovery

Strategies designed to accelerate tissue regeneration, stimulate venous return, and clear fatigue after intense training blocks, demanding workouts, or competitions.

Tools we may incorporate:

  • Sequential pressotherapy Graduated pneumatic compression to facilitate circulatory drainage and clear accumulated metabolic waste.
  • Manual lymphatic drainage Gentle, rhythmic manual techniques designed to decongest overloaded tissues and relieve heaviness.
  • Photobiomodulation / Red light Specific therapeutic wavelengths that stimulate cellular energy production and support natural tissue repair.
02

Body goal

Decongestion

Targeted manual release work designed to ease muscle stiffness, release deep contractures, and clear fatigue across major muscle groups.

Tools we may incorporate:

  • Therapeutic deep-tissue massage Focused, deep manual manoeuvres targeting areas of postural or athletic strain to restore muscle suppleness.
  • Myofascial release Precise manual pressure on fascial restrictions and trigger points to release adhesions and restore tissue mobility.
03

Body goal

Activation

Neuromuscular and physical readiness before events, competitions, or intense efforts, waking muscle tone without creating premature fatigue.

Tools we may incorporate:

  • Guided joint mobility Assisted sequences to open movement ranges, lubricate articular joints, and prepare the body for exertion.
  • Neuromuscular stimulation (Power Plate) Calibrated vibration to enhance microcirculation, motor unit recruitment, and proprioception.
04

Body goal

Care

Sessions dedicated to regular maintenance, nervous system decompression, and proactive health—building an ongoing routine that cares for your body before discomfort appears.

Tools we may incorporate:

  • Circulatory and restorative massage Calm, steady pacing to settle the nervous system, release built-up tension, and promote restful recovery.
  • Radiofrequency (INDIBA / TECAR) Deep targeted diathermy to boost vascularisation, oxygenate tissues, and stimulate cellular regeneration.
  • Lifestyle and rest guidelines Personalised hydration, recovery, and mobility strategies to prolong the benefits of your session.

Common profiles

Who is it for?

Targeted body care is valuable at different times and activity levels, whether you train at a high level or simply want a regular health routine.

04

Key situations where structured bodywork makes a real difference to your daily life.

01

You train regularly

You want to improve how your body recovers between workouts.

For runners, cyclists, or strength trainees looking to assimilate training volume, reduce lingering muscular fatigue, and perform fresher in every session.

02

You compete or face high-demand weeks

You want to embed structured recovery into your schedule.

During peak athletic training or periods of heavy work pressure and postural strain, releasing deep tension and encouraging venous return.

03

You are in a recovery process

You want to complement your physiotherapy work.

We coordinate muscle release and drainage with your clinical recovery, supporting tissue healing without interfering with your primary physiotherapy treatment.

04

You want proactive body care

You want to introduce recovery and physical care into your routine.

For those spending long hours sitting or standing, dealing with stiffness in their back and legs, or simply choosing to give their body a conscious, quality pause.
