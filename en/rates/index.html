Packages: terms & conditions

Session packages can be combined across Physiotherapy, Strength, Pilates, and Stretching, allowing you to tailor each session to your immediate needs.

Valid exclusively for in-clinic sessions, valid for 2 months from date of purchase, and payable in full upon purchase. Packages are personal and non-transferable, and non-refundable once started (a package is considered started once the first session is used).

Surcharges

Evening surcharge for sessions starting from 8:00 PM: +€20.

Weekend and public holiday surcharge: +€20.

Surcharges are cumulative and also apply to sessions used with a package, payable separately at the time of the session.

Bookings and cancellations

Cancellations must be communicated at least 24 hours in advance on weekdays (Monday to Friday, excluding holidays) or 48 hours on weekends and public holidays.

Late cancellations and no-shows without prior notice are considered completed sessions, except in certified medical or force majeure circumstances.

Travel conditions

Travel within Sitges and up to 10 km is included. Beyond this distance, a €10 surcharge is added for each additional 10 km outbound stretch, calculated via Google Maps.

  • Sitges + 10 km included
  • +€10 per additional 10 km outbound

Home visits include travel within a 10 km radius from Avinguda Camí dels Capellans, 79 (local 3), Sitges. The total session price, including travel, is confirmed and agreed upon prior to booking confirmation.

Evening surcharge for sessions starting from 8:00 PM: +€20.

Weekend and public holiday surcharge: +€20.

Surcharges are cumulative and added to the session price.

Same minimum cancellation notice as in-clinic: 24h on weekdays or 48h on weekends and holidays.

Home visit sessions are contracted individually and paid prior to each session, including applicable surcharges. In-clinic session packages cannot be redeemed for home visits.
